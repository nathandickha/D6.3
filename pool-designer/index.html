FAQ

Frequently asked

Do I need to install any software?+

No. The studio runs in a modern web browser.

Can I save my design and come back to it?+

Saved-design functionality can be connected to your preferred account or database system.

Does the designer work on mobile?+

Yes, although larger screens provide the most precise design experience.

Can my pool builder use my design?+

Yes. Designs can be shared as a clear visual reference for further professional review.
